# Tyme Privacy Policy


**Effective date:** November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information **Tyme**, an internal
application operated by **Datavyne LLC** ("Datavyne", "we", "us"), collects,
how it is used and how it is protected. Tyme is used only by
Datavyne's employees, owners and independent contractors; it is not offered
to the public.

## 1. Information we collect

**About Users** (from Datavyne's administrators and from Google sign-in):

- name, work email address and the Google account identifier used to sign in;
- role in Tyme (worker, approver, payroll, administrator), worker type
  (hourly employee, salaried employee, contractor), approver, start and end
  dates;
- pay rates, with their effective dates;
- QuickBooks Online vendor or employee identifiers.

**Time and payment records:** hours by day, project and type, notes,
submission and approval history, overtime explanations, corrections, pay
calculations, payment dates, payment methods and payment references.

**Technical records:** for security and audit purposes, the IP address and
browser of each change and sign-in, and a log of emails sent.

**We do not collect or store** Social Security numbers, taxpayer
identification numbers, bank account numbers, tax forms (such as W-9s) or
payment card details. Those stay in Datavyne's accounting and payroll
system (QuickBooks Online).

## 2. QuickBooks Online data

When a Datavyne administrator connects Tyme to Datavyne's QuickBooks
Online company, Tyme uses Intuit's API to:

- read the company name, the list of expense accounts, and the names and
  status of the vendors and employees whose QuickBooks identifiers are
  entered in Tyme;
- create bills (for contractors) and time entries (for hourly employees)
  from approved timesheets;
- read those bills and their linked bill payments, to learn when a bill has
  been paid.

Tyme does not read other QuickBooks data, and does not change or
delete anything in QuickBooks other than creating the bills and time
entries described above. The access tokens Intuit issues are stored
encrypted. An administrator can disconnect QuickBooks at any time, which
revokes Tyme's access.

## 3. How we use information

Only to run Datavyne's business: recording and approving time, calculating
pay, paying contractors and employees, sending related emails (for example
"timesheet approved" or "payment sent"), meeting legal recordkeeping duties,
and keeping the application secure. We do not sell information or use it
for advertising.

## 4. Who can see it

- **Inside Datavyne:** each User sees their own records. Approvers see the
  time of the people they approve; payroll staff and administrators see
  what their role requires. Approvers do not see pay.
- **Service providers** that run parts of Tyme for us: Google (sign-in
  and email), Cloudflare (secure network access) and Intuit (QuickBooks
  Online). They handle information only to provide their service.
- **When required by law**, for example to answer a lawful request or a
  wage-and-hour audit.

## 5. Retention

Time, approval and payment records are kept for at least three years, as
federal wage-and-hour rules require, and longer where Datavyne's
recordkeeping needs or the law require. Tyme never deletes these
records; it keeps a history of every change. Backups are kept for disaster
recovery under Datavyne's backup practices.

## 6. Security

Sign-in uses Datavyne's Google Workspace accounts with 2-Step Verification,
and the application sits behind Cloudflare Access. Traffic is encrypted in
transit. QuickBooks tokens are encrypted at rest. Access inside the
application is limited by role and checked on every request, and every
change is recorded in an audit log.

## 7. Your choices

Users can see their own records in Tyme and can ask an administrator
to correct inaccurate information. Because time and payment records are
legal business records, corrections are made by adding a new, logged entry
rather than erasing the original.
